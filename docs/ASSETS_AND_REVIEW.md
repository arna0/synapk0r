# Asset provenance and professional review

The project owner confirmed on 26 November 2026 that permission for the scanned hand and Renderpeople Nathan character has not been confirmed, and that a practising clinician has not reviewed the Doctor module.

## Current release assets

Characters and first-person hands are generated by `frontend/js/characters.js` from Three.js primitives. Their walking, seated poses and reactions are handled by `npc.js`. Rooms and equipment use the existing procedural scene code. The four scanned-model files have been removed from current release content. They remain in earlier Git history and earlier published builds.

`node scripts/check-assets.mjs` checks browser content and synchronized Flutter assets for the removed models. CI and mobile synchronization run this check. Third-party library licenses remain applicable to bundled libraries.

## Source license

The repository currently has no owner-selected source-code license. Contributors retain their existing rights. The owners should choose and approve a license before advertising the repository as licensed open source. This document grants no rights on their behalf.

## Doctor review

The Doctor module is an unreviewed, simplified career-exploration exercise. Its score measures the simulated actions. A practising clinician must review the complaints, observations, escalation choices, patient communication and feedback before clinical-training claims are made.

Record the reviewer's name with permission, qualifications, review date, scenario version, corrections and approval scope here after review. Current status is **awaiting review**.

## Learning validation

For the next pilot, record first-attempt results, assistance used, completion and a different-case independent replay. Ask learners to explain the evidence behind their decision. Compare their transfer to a fresh case and discuss the results with a qualified teacher. The existing seven-person study remains early usability evidence.
